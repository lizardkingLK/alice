# Sign in with email

Use your work email and password to open Alice.

**Audience:** All users

---

## Steps

1. Open your workspace URL (the link your admin shared).
2. Go to **Sign in** (or `/login`).
3. Enter your **email** and **password**.
4. Select **Sign in**.

If your credentials are correct and your email is on the allowlist, you'll be
taken to the **Dashboard**.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                      | What to do                                                                          |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Access denied" or similar | Your email may not be on the allowlist — see [Request access](./request-access.md). |
| Wrong password             | Use [Forgot password](./forgot-password.md).                                        |
| Account deactivated        | Contact an admin — they can reactivate you under **Users**.                         |
| Email not confirmed        | Check your inbox for a confirmation link, then try again.                           |
