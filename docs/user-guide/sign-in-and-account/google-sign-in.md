# Sign in with Google

Sign in with your Google account when your workspace has Google OAuth enabled.

**Audience:** All users

---

## Before you start

- Use a Google account whose **email matches the allowlist** (domain or exact
  email entry).
- If Google sign-in is disabled for your deployment, use
  [Sign in with email](./email-sign-in.md) instead.

---

## Steps

1. Open your workspace URL.
2. Go to **Sign in**.
3. Select **Sign in with Google**.
4. Choose the Google account you want to use.
5. Approve access if Google prompts you.

On success you'll land on the **Dashboard**. If your email isn't allowlisted,
you'll see an access-denied page instead.

---

## Linking Google to an existing account

If you already use email/password with the same address, signing in with Google
for that email uses the same Alice profile once admission checks pass.
