# Request access

Ask to join Alice when sign-in shows an access-denied page.

**Audience:** All users (before admission)

---

## When to use this

You see **access denied** (or can't complete sign-in) because your email isn't
on the workspace **allowlist** yet. Use the contact form to send an access
request to admins.

---

## Steps

1. On the access-denied page, open **Contact** (or go to `/contact`).
2. Choose subject **Access request** (or equivalent wording).
3. Enter your **name** and **work email**.
4. Briefly explain why you need access.
5. Submit the form.

Admins review requests under **Users → Requests**. If approved, they'll add your
email to the allowlist and you can sign in normally.

---

## Limits

To reduce spam, each email can submit at most **3** access requests within a
**30-day** rolling window.

---

## After you're approved

- Sign in with [email](./email-sign-in.md) or [Google](./google-sign-in.md).
- You may still need to be added to individual **projects** before you see all
  work — ask your project owner or admin.
