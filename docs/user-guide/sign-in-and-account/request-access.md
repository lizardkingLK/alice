# Request access

Ask to join Alice when sign-up or Google shows an access-denied page.

**Audience:** All users (before admission)

---

## When to use this

Your email isn't on the workspace **allowlist** yet. Use **Sign up** or
**Google** — if admission fails, you'll see **access denied** with a request
form so admins know who you are and which **projects** you need.

**Sign in** with an unknown email does **not** open this form (you'll see
invalid credentials). Use Sign up / Google to request access, or
[Contact](/contact) if you already asked and need to follow up.

---

## Steps

### From the access-denied page

1. Try **Sign up** or **Google** with your work email.
2. If you aren't allowlisted, open the access-denied form.
3. Confirm your **work email** (and name if you want).
4. Optionally enter **project keys** (comma-separated), e.g. `ACME, BETA`.
5. Briefly explain why you need access (include project **names** if you don't
   know the keys).
6. Select **Request access**.

### From Contact

1. Go to `/contact` (or open **Contact** from the site / sign-in page).
2. Choose subject **Access request**.
3. Optionally enter **project keys**.
4. Enter your email, message, and submit.

Admins review requests under **Users → Requests**. If approved, they'll add your
email to the allowlist (often with the projects you named) and you can complete
sign-in.

---

## Limits

To reduce spam, each email can submit at most **3** access requests within a
**30-day** rolling window. Submitting again within about an hour updates your
latest message and project keys without counting as a new submission.

---

## After you're approved

- Check your email for an **invite** (or use
  [Forgot password](./forgot-password.md)) to **set a password** before signing
  in with email/password. A wrong password on sign-in shows **Invalid email or
  password** on the sign-in page (same as a mistype).
- Then sign in with [email](./email-sign-in.md) or [Google](./google-sign-in.md).
- Guest allowlist access is limited to the projects the admin selected. Ask an
  admin or project owner if you need more projects or full membership.
