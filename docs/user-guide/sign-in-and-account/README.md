## Before you sign in

Your organization uses an **admission allowlist**. You can only sign in if:

- Your email domain is allowlisted (e.g. `@company.com`), **or**
- Your exact email address was added to the allowlist by an admin.

If you're not allowlisted, use [Request access](./request-access.md) or contact
your workspace admin.

---

## New users

If an admin **invited** you by email, open the invitation link, set your
password on the reset page, then you'll land in the workspace.

If you **self-register** with email, you may need to confirm your email before
the first sign-in completes.
