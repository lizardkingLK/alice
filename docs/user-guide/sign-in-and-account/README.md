## Before you sign in

Your organization uses an **admission allowlist**. You can only sign in if:

- Your email domain is allowlisted (e.g. `@company.com`), **or**
- Your exact email address was added to the allowlist by an admin.

If you're not allowlisted, use [Request access](./request-access.md) or contact
your workspace admin.

---

## New users

If an admin **invited** you by email, open the invitation link, set your
password on the reset page, then you'll land in the workspace.

If you **self-register** with email, you may need to confirm your email before
the first sign-in completes.

## After you sign in

Visiting **Home** (`/`) while signed in opens a profile-style launchpad that
fills the first viewport under the top nav: solid product-green cover (or your
cover photo), avatar, name, handle, role, a **Dashboard** button, and your
projects (name plus description) on a soft grey productivity surface. The main
column greets you by time of day (small mono text) and — when you have them —
lists up to five open assigned work items (liquid-glass card, **% completed**
pill) and up to five unread notifications (green unread dots, red count badge,
humanized times). Empty lists stay hidden. Role-based **Quick links** always
sit in the right column (for example admins see create project and manage
users). Scroll for a full-height footer (**Product Development Automated**)
with About, Contact, and workspace links — those About/Contact links are hidden
from the signed-in top nav. Open **Dashboard** (or the footer Overview link) to
get back into the workspace.
