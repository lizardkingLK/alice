# Comments and activity

Discuss work on the item and review what changed.

**Audience:** All users

---

## Discussion tab

1. Open a work item.
2. Select the **Discussion** tab.

Use the rich-text composer to add comments. You can:

- **@mention** teammates
- Use quick replies where shown (e.g. "Looks good!", "Need help?")
- Submit with **Ctrl/⌘+Enter**
- Press **M** to focus the composer from elsewhere on the page

Mentions and replies can generate [notifications](../notifications/dashboard-inbox.md).

---

## Activity tab

The **Activity** tab shows field changes and status updates over time.

If the tab shows placeholder text, your workspace may still be rolling out the
full activity timeline — check back after upgrades.

---

## Work log tab

The **Work log** tab tracks time or notes logged against the item (when enabled
for your project). Done items may restrict new log entries.
