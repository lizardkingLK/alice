# Calendar view

Plan and reschedule work by due date on a month grid.

**Audience:** All users

---

## Open calendar

1. Select **Board** in the sidebar.
2. Open the **Calendar** tab (`/board?tab=calendar`).

---

## What you see

- A **month grid** with items placed on their **due date**
- An **unscheduled** panel for items without due dates (when shown)

---

## Change due dates

Drag an item from the unscheduled list onto a day, or drag between days on the
grid, to update its due date.

Open an item from the calendar to edit other fields on the detail page.

---

## Tips

- Combine calendar with board filters for project or assignee focus.
- Items without due dates won't appear on the grid until scheduled.
